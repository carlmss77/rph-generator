Privacy policy

Last updated 23 November 2026

RPH Generator helps teachers make their weekly lesson plans (RPH) from their own module files. This page explains what the app does with your information.

What we get when you sign in

When you sign in with Google, the app receives your name and email address. They are used only to show who is signed in and to keep your files apart from other teachers' files.

Your Google Drive

The app asks for the drive.file permission. This lets it see and change only the files it creates itself, kept in a folder called "RPH Generator" in your own Google Drive. It cannot see any other file in your Drive.

Your module files, past RPH, timetable, holidays and generated RPH are saved in that folder. They are not stored on our server and are not shared with anyone. You can delete the folder at any time.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell, rent or share your information with anyone.
  • We do not use your information for advertising.
  • We do not use your files to train AI models.

The app's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Cookies

The app uses one sign-in cookie to keep you signed in. It does not use tracking or advertising cookies.

Removing access

You can sign out at any time. To remove the app's access completely, go to myaccount.google.com/permissions and remove RPH Generator.

How to use RPH Generator

A short guide for teachers. Setup takes about 5 minutes, once a year. After that, each week takes a few seconds.

Before you start · Sign in · Setup (once a year) · Every week · Holidays · On your phone · Problems

Before you start

Have these files ready on your laptop:

  • Your module files, the ready-made lesson plans, e.g. MODULE 1-GET SMART.doc to MODULE 10-GET SMART.doc. Word files (.doc or .docx). The module number must be in each file name.
  • One weekly RPH you already made, e.g. M41.docx. Pick a full week with every day filled in. The app copies its format and reads your timetable from it.

A laptop is easiest for setup. You can make weekly RPH on your phone later.

1. Sign in

  1. Open the app and press Sign in with Google.
  2. Choose your Google account.
  3. When Google asks, tick the box for Google Drive and press Continue. The app can only see the files it makes itself, in a folder called RPH Generator in your Drive.

2. Setup (once a year)

The home page shows three steps. Do them in order. A step turns green when it is done.

Step 1: Upload your module files

  1. Press Upload modules.
  2. Select all your module files at once (hold Ctrl and click each one, or press Ctrl+A in the folder).
  3. Press Read lessons and wait. Old .doc files can take up to a minute.
  4. Press See lessons to check that every module and lesson is there.

Step 2: Upload one past weekly RPH

  1. Press Upload past RPH and choose your file, e.g. M41.docx.
  2. Press Use this file.

Step 3: Check your timetable

The app fills this page in from your past RPH. Check each part, fix anything wrong, then press Save.

  • Week numbering: type any one week number and the date of its Monday (for example, this week's number and this Monday). Every other week is counted from it.
  • English periods each week: one row per period, with day, class and time. Use + Add period or Remove to change it.
  • Where each class is up to: the last lesson each class finished at the end of a week. Each class then moves on one lesson every period. If a class skips or repeats a lesson, add a new row for that week.
  • Topic for each module and Reflection line for each class: the words printed in your RPH. Change them if you like.

3. Every week

  1. Open the app. The home page shows a box with the week number already filled in.
  2. Check the week number, then press Make RPH. It takes a few seconds.
  3. Press Open PDF to view or print it, or Download Word to edit it.
  4. After teaching, change the reflection numbers in the Word file if you need to.

Every RPH is also saved in the RPH Generator folder in your Google Drive. Your last weeks are listed under Recent weeks on the home page.

Holidays

  1. Press Holidays at the top.
  2. Press + Add day, pick the date and type the name, e.g. Cuti Deepavali. For a holiday of several days, add one row per day with the same name.
  3. Optional: choose a picture (PNG or JPG) to show as the holiday page.
  4. Press Save holidays.

No lesson is planned on a holiday, and classes do not move forward that day. Add holidays before you make that week's RPH. If you already made it, just press Make RPH again.

On your phone

Open the same address in your phone browser and sign in with the same Google account. The tabs are at the bottom of the screen. Tip: use "Add to Home screen" in your browser menu to open it like an app.

Problems

The app takes about a minute to open.
It sleeps when nobody has used it for a while. Wait a moment and it wakes up.
"The app needs permission to save files in your Google Drive."
Sign in again and tick the Google Drive box.
A class is on the wrong lesson.
Open Timetable, add a row under "Where each class is up to" with the right lesson for last week, press Save, then make the RPH again.
My timetable changed.
Open Timetable, change the periods, press Save.
I have a new set of modules.
Open Lessons, upload the new files and choose "Replace all my lessons". Then check Timetable.
